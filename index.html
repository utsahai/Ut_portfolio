Featured Project

Batch Tracking System

Manufacturing plants for an animal health client were relying on manual SAP lookups to track batches — slow, and prone to error. I partnered with plant stakeholders, the ETL team, and the product owner to scope exactly what needed tracking, then led end-to-end delivery of a real-time system on FastAPI, Neo4j, and PostgreSQL. It cut data retrieval time by 30% and now runs across 12 of 21 manufacturing facilities, monitoring 4 million+ items.

Featured Project

TLM Chatbot

An internal Q&A chatbot for stakeholders at an automotive client — meant to let them look up their own applications' status and info directly — was underused because it only answered questions and didn't help anyone get work done. I identified the gap and pivoted it into a self-service Total Lifecycle Management tool, adding in-chat incident creation and automated data-gathering for resource deployment, patching, and migration. The redesign cut team workload by 35%.
